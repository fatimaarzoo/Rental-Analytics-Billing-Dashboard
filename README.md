# RentDesk : Rental Analytics & Billing Dashboard

A comprehensive web-based Tenant Management System that helps property owners and administrators manage tenants, rooms, rent collection, utility bills, expenses, and financial reports efficiently.

## Features

### Tenant Management
- Add, update, view, and delete tenant records.
- Store tenant information including contact details and occupancy status.
- Assign tenants to rooms.
- Maintain tenant history and records.

### Room Management
- Create and manage rooms.
- Track room availability and occupancy.
- Assign and reassign rooms to tenants.
- View room-wise tenant information.

### Billing Management
- Generate monthly rent bills.
- Record utility charges such as electricity, water, and maintenance fees.
- Automatically calculate total payable amounts.
- Track bill payment status.

### Expense Management
- Record property-related expenses.
- Categorize expenses.
- Monitor monthly and yearly expenditures.

### Dashboard & Analytics
- Revenue and expense overview.
- Occupancy statistics.
- Profit and loss analysis.
- Interactive charts and visualizations.
